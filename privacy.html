Privacy policy

Last updated 28 October 2026

This website

This site has no cookies, analytics, forms or tracking. It does not collect any personal information.

The "Final Sixty Analytics" Google app

Final Sixty Analytics is a private tool used only by the owner of the Final Sixty YouTube channel. It is not offered to the public.

  • What it accesses: with the channel owner's permission, it reads the Final Sixty channel's own YouTube data and YouTube Analytics reports (views, watch time, audience retention, traffic sources, subscriber changes). It uses read-only permissions only (youtube.readonly and yt-analytics.readonly), so it cannot upload, edit or delete anything.
  • How the data is used: only to understand how the channel's videos perform and to improve future videos.
  • Where it is kept: on the channel owner's own computer. The sign-in token is stored in the computer's secure keychain.
  • Sharing: the data is not sold, shared or transferred to anyone, and is not used for advertising.
  • Removing access: access can be withdrawn at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Viewers

Final Sixty does not collect personal information from viewers. YouTube's own privacy policy covers watching the videos.
